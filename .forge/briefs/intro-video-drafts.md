# Drafts: LUX intro video (three angles)

All three: 1080×1080, 30fps, muted-first captions, lux-v3 palette, real captured screens in a minimal glass phone frame, Figtree with an Instrument Serif italic accent word.

---

## Angle A: "The case file". The investigation as a visual structure

**One line:** Stage the video like an evidence board. The user's reaction is the case, their products are the "possible contributors", and LUX lays out evidence for and against. It's distinctive and mirrors the product's own vocabulary.

| t | Beat | On screen | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | A close-up crop of a cheek photo (gallery image) under a translucent sage veil. Caption: "Day 3. Redness, cheeks." Handwritten-style tick marks. | Slow push-in; caption types in with a caret. |
| 3–6s | Problem | Seven product photos fan out like evidence cards pinned to a board. Caption: "Seven products. *Which one?*" | Cards drop and settle with a slight rotate; thin red string lines draw between them. |
| 6–10s | Brand | The strings converge to the centre and become the orb. The LUX wordmark sweeps light. "Investigate your skin." | The strings retract into the orb; the orb's mark assembles. |
| 10–16s | Exhibit 01: the map | Phone with step 1. The face diagram pops out large and the region pills light up in order. Caption: "Exhibit 01 — *where* it shows up." | The card lifts out of the phone (scale 1 → 1.6, shadow grows), and the regions blink on in a 60ms stagger. |
| 16–22s | Exhibit 02: the timeline | Products screen: buckets "Long-term / Recent / New addition". Caption: "Exhibit 02 — *when* each product started." | Bucket rows slide in as file tabs. |
| 22–32s | The analysis | The analysis screen. The "Best fit so far" card is pulled out. Captions: "Evidence *for*. Evidence *against*." Then: "A possible contributor — not a verdict." | Two columns sort chips left (for) and right (against); the best-fit card lands centre. |
| 32–42s | Follow-up | Progress: the trend line draws severe → mild; calendar discs pop in. Caption: "Pause one product. *Watch* what changes." | The line draws with a dot riding it; the day counter ticks 1 → 19. |
| 42–50s | Close | Board pulls back. All exhibits float as cards; they collapse into the orb. Lockup: "LUX — find what your skin is reacting to." | Camera pull-back, then everything converges and the orb breathes. |

**Risk:** the detective metaphor (red string, "exhibit") can read gimmicky, true-crime, off the calm brand. "Suspects" is avoided, but red string still implies blame.

## Angle C: "Design principles". Type-led manifesto, UI as proof

**One line:** Aimed squarely at the hiring designer. Four big editorial principles (motionsites-style type), each proved by a 4-second UI moment. The video sells the *thinking*, and the screens are evidence.

| t | Beat | On screen | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Hook | Black-teal → sage gradient. "Skincare apps give you a *score.*" → strike → "LUX gives you a *reason.*" | The strike draws left → right; the swap is a blur crossfade. |
| 4–8s | Brand | Orb and wordmark lockup over the canvas. "An investigation, not a verdict." | Orb assembly; the light sweep. |
| 8–16s | Principle 1 — "Ask only what changes the answer." | Step 1 face map in the phone; region chips light. | The type slides up out; the phone rises in; chips stagger. |
| 16–24s | Principle 2 — "Time is the evidence." | Products by bucket (Long-term / Recent / New). | The bucket rows draw a timeline axis under them. |
| 24–34s | Principle 3 — "Show evidence *for* and *against*." | The analysis: best fit, could-go-either-way chips, evidence rows. | Cards fan out like the motionsites "Reverie" cards, three tilted panels. |
| 34–44s | Principle 4 — "Calm by design." | Progress: trend line, calendar, gallery. | Slow, deliberate draws; the text is smaller and quieter. |
| 44–52s | Close | "Designed and built by Yeon Ji Lee." Lockup. | The cards collapse into the orb. |

**Risk:** principles-as-slides can feel like a talk deck. Less of a single-user thread (it weakens criterion 5). The "score" jab conflicts with CHECK's own % score cards in the same app.

## FINAL — B revised (after grader round 1)

**Steals:**
- from A: "A possible contributor — not a verdict." and the for/against beat;
- from C: "Time is the evidence." and the timeline axis drawing under the product buckets, plus the credit line "Designed and built by Yeon Ji Lee".

**Fixes:**
- the hook states the situation by 2.5s;
- "diagnosis" is gone;
- no orbit, tilt or light-sweep decoration: entrances are straight rises, from blur;
- the tracking beat is framed as *keeping the record*, not as proof (no causation by montage);
- the loop returns to an empty canvas.

**Type:** Urbanist only. Light dark ink `#2E2A3F`, with key words Bold in `#313560`. Every caption sits on the canvas, not on glass. Sizes:
- headline 84–96px;
- supporting line 48px;
- kicker 32px tracked caps (a label like "DAY 1", always shown with a ≥48px caption).

**Ground:** the canvas gradient `#E2EDF1` → `#D3E4E7` → `#B1CAD2` at 165°, with a very slow drift of the gradient's position. No glows, no dark.

**Phone:** a minimal panel-glass rounded frame with a 14px bezel, showing real 390-wide captures at about 1.1×. It may run off the bottom edge.

**Motion:** ease-out `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` for entrances and ease-in-out for handovers. Nothing bounces. Text resolves from 16px blur and a 24px rise, staggered 70ms per word.

## V2 — "Less" (2 Oct 2026, landscape 1920×1080, ~50s)

**Why a second version:** v1 walks through the screens in order, and the product's thesis (use less, find what to take out, never add) only lands in the last line. V2 builds the whole arc around subtraction, so the picture itself goes from many products to fewer.

| t (s) | Beat | Copy | Picture / motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5.5 | The pile | "Your skin flared up, / so you added **another** product. / And another." | Product tiles drop into a grid on the right, faster and faster, until 14 of them crowd the frame. |
| 5.5–11 | The turn | "What if your skin needs **less**?" → orb + "LUX helps you find **what to take out**." | The pile blurs and sinks back as depth; the statement resolves centre; the orb rises. |
| 11–17 | Map | (v1 copy) | The v1 walkthrough map beat. |
| 17–21.5 | Products | (v1 copy) | The v1 products beat, with the then → now axis. |
| 21.5–37 | Analysis + evidence | (v1 copy) | The v1 analysis and evidence beats; the products fly out of the phone. |
| 37–44 | Take one out | "Pause one product, / then note **what happens**." | The five-product routine sits in a row. The new addition is ringed, lifts out and ghosts to "Paused", and the rest close the gap. A week of check-in dots fills underneath. |
| 44–50 | Close | Logo + "Use **less**. You don't need more." | v1 close. |

The arc runs from 14 products at the start to 4 at the end.
