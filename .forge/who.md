## The business
LUX is a portfolio project, not a company: an AI-guided skincare **investigation** app (lux-v3, Next.js) that helps someone work out which products they already use may be associated with a skin reaction. Source docs: `docs/product-brief.md`, `../lux-scope-decision.md`, `AGENTS.md`.

## What we sell / make
Judgement, shown through one well-made product. Artefacts include the live prototype, the design system (`docs/design.md` / `design.html`), the Figma file and motion pieces (`../videos/`).

## What we never do
- Claim LUX diagnoses anything or names a cause. The product brief's controlled vocabulary applies to the video too. Use "associated with" and "possible contributor". Never use "caused your reaction", "allergy diagnosis", "toxic ingredient" or "safe for you".
- Show a scientific-looking percentage on the analysis. CompatCard's % on Check is the one sanctioned score.
- Use solid white surfaces, or drop the v2 gradients (standing visual rule).
- Inflate scope. "Complexity is not the currency. Judgement is." (scope decision §1)
