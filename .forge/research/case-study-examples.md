# What makes a good case study: research notes (6 Oct 2026)

## What the research says
- **The first 30 seconds decide it.** In 2025 a single entry-level UX post pulled 500–800 applicants. The cold read needs three things: what was broken, one real decision and why, and what changed. *(MentorCruise, "Entry-level UX designer jobs in 2026")*
- **Treat it like a trailer, not the movie.** Aim for 10 minutes of reading at most, and keep the text-to-visual ratio around 40/60. If it runs longer, split it. The full story belongs in the interview. *(Matej Latin, UX Collective, "Only 30 seconds to reject your portfolio?")*
- **Show the end result first and tell a story, not a process.** Use scannable headings and answer "why". *(UX Tools, "5 Principles of Exceptional Case Studies")*
- **A personal opening pulls readers in.** UXfolio's best mock-project example opens with the designer's own food allergies. LUX can do the same with your skin. *(UXfolio, "6 Excellent Product Designer Case Studies")*
- **"Showcases" are the shorter, more visual alternative** to long case studies: highlight the skills and the result, and let visuals carry the process. *(Femke van Schoonhoven, "Showcases: the new portfolio case study"; FolioLinked)*
