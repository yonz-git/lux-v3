# Audience

Status: DRAFTED BY CLAUDE, 2 Oct 2026. *Unconfirmed*, taken from `../lux-scope-decision.md` §1 ("a portfolio piece competing for attention from a hiring designer").

## Who they are
A design lead or senior product designer at a product company, hiring a mid-level product designer or design engineer. They skim 30–60 portfolios a week, mostly on a laptop between meetings, sometimes on a phone from a LinkedIn or Read.cv link. They give a case study about 10 seconds before deciding whether to scroll.

## What they've already tried
They've seen every Dribbble-glass skincare concept, every "AI assistant" chat mock and every 40-screen case study with no reasoning in it.

## What they're allergic to
- Scope inflation and feature lists.
- Stock-template motion: random parallax, spinning 3D and Envato transitions.
- Unreadable UI flashed for half a second.
- Hype copy ("revolutionary AI").
- Medical overclaim.

## The belief that stops them acting
"Pretty UI is cheap now, since AI tools make it. Can this person actually *think* about a product and *ship* it?" The video has to show a defended decision and real, built craft, not just polish.

## What winning looks like for them
Within the first 5 seconds they understand the problem LUX takes on. By the end they've seen one clear idea executed beautifully and in motion, and they click through to the case study or prototype.

## Secondary audiences (if any)
Peers on LinkedIn or Instagram, where the 1:1 format autoplays muted. Design them for silent autoplay too.
