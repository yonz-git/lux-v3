Do's and Don'ts

  • ✓Take every value from a token, a role or a component; a new look means a new token first.
  • ✓Keep one primary action per screen, and make "selected" the primary colour every time.
  • ✓Use glass for controls and panels for boxes, each with its edge and top highlight.
  • ✓Pair every status colour with a word.
  • ✓Give every control a target of at least 44 and a visible focus outline.
  • ✓Check at 390/440 and 1440, and measure contrast on the real pixels.
  • ✗No opaque white fill — not on a tag, a button, a card or a close button.
  • ✗No gradients beyond the canvas and the primary; no glows, no dark ground.
  • ✗No text below 12px, no weight outside the five loaded.
  • ✗No glass on glass, no solid card on the canvas.
  • ✗No inline icons on a screen and no width/height on an icon.
  • ✗No percentage or score on the investigation analysis; scores are for compatibility checks only.
